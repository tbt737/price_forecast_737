## 2. Why the rejection does not depend on the data
- **Wrong product.** The model emits a dimensionless count, never a price displacement. The lens records that it emits no displacement, no permanent impact and no direction, and that a daily price-forecast platform needs `E[p_{t+h}]`, which this model cannot produce even in principle (`ev:…/galperin/lens_results.md:45-46`).
- **Wrong time scale.** The report defines the collisions as the peaks and bottoms of tick-by-tick price data (`ev:sources/Econophysics Market Microstructure Architecture.md:19`), inside one absorption episode that terminates — a market has no such terminus, and the report never specifies a counting window (`lens_results.md:113`). This platform holds one bar per day: the stored price observation grain is a `DATE` (`fact_price_daily.price_date`, `db/migrations/001_core_schema.sql:237`), and there is no intraday table and no intraday observation timestamp on the price path (`lens_results.md:159`); the ingestion and audit stamps `ingested_at`, `created_at` and `updated_at` (`:250-252`) are not price observations. The stored measure is `frac_pred_gt_window = mean(N_pred > K-1)` (`opus_final_verify.py:141`). On futures at threshold 10 / `K`=10 it is 1.0, and at threshold 3 / `K`=40 it is 0.0677, rounded to four decimal places from 0.06766917293233082 (`opus_results.json`, `FUTURES.A_fixed_window`).
- **The source mapping fails before any data.** Five findings of the report's own audit lens, adjudicated in §7: `lens_results.md:13,21,24,30,33` (sha16 `a59f5a43a0651bee`).
- **Arithmetic error.** `floor(pi/theta)` (`audit_galperin_math.py:82-83`) is wrong exactly where `pi/theta` is an integer; the universal formula is `ceil(pi/theta) - 1` (`:86-88`). Exact rational simulation covers the integer-mass collision table only — `:101` enables exact mode for integer masses with `m2 <= 10_000` — so "reproduced by exact simulation" holds for that table and not beyond. Section 3.2's decay law is correct (`:11-12`); the §5.3 pseudocode implementing it (`:13`) drops the `cos^2` factor and inverts the role of `gamma` (`lens_results.md:304-305`). The formula's only input is the mass ratio (`:82-88`), so the predicted count `N` is independent of shock velocity; that does not establish that elapsed execution time, or any price effect outside the count, is velocity-independent.
- **Structural, specific to this platform.** At HEAD `configs/commodities/` holds 52 committed profiles. Thirty are VN equities in the ENTRADE basket (`configs/ingestion/sources.yaml:188-217`) and ten are Yahoo futures/ETF lines that could carry volume (`:11-21`); the remaining **twelve lie outside both baskets**. Four of the twelve are Vietnamese domestic spot codes — `GOLD_VN`, `SILVER_VN`, `PEPPER_VN`, `DIESEL_VN` (`:126-143`) — and their collector carries no quantity at HEAD: every entry of `PARSERS` in `etl/sources/market/vn_domestic.py:146` is typed to return a single `float | None` price, and the record built at `:176-185` has no volume key. Of the other eight, five (`CHINESE_GARLIC`, `INDIAN_CHILIES`, `PEANUTS`, `RED_ONION_INDIA`, `ROBUSTA`) are priced off Agmarknet national-median CSV mappings (`configs/ingestion/csv_imports.yaml`, instrument `IN_NATIONAL_MEDIAN` at `:89,99,112,122,135,145,159,169,213,223`), and three (`DEHYDRATED_GARLIC`, `DEHYDRATED_ONION`, `RED_ONION_CHINA`) have no configured price feed at all; the two dehydrated profiles state "NO futures and NO free daily price feed … profile is config-only for now" (`configs/commodities/dehydrated_onion.yaml:68`, `dehydrated_garlic.yaml:66`). This is what the configuration establishes: those twelve have no volume-bearing feed configured today. It does not establish that volume could not be obtained for them. The two commodities carrying the largest measured forecast edge in the repo's own regime test — `INDIAN_CHILIES` and `ROBUSTA` — sit inside that group of twelve, and the repo records the deciding axis as commodity liquidity and type, liquid futures behaving like a random walk (`docs/ml/rejected_experiments.md:27-33`). `ROBUSTA`'s instruments are ICE_RC, Dak Lak spot, the ICO indicator and an Indian proxy (`configs/commodities/robusta.yaml:8,14,20,26`), none of which appears in the Yahoo basket. **The experiment's `ROBUSTA_ARABICA` futures series is not this commodity** — `fetch_yahoo.py:15` maps that label to the `KC=F` ticker (ICE Coffee "C", Arabica), which is absent from `sources.yaml`.

## 4. Limits of this refutation
- Symbol universe was chosen with full-sample knowledge. - The `data/` futures basket named above, not the frozen one, is what t4, v4, v4b and the adversarial scripts read.

- The power argument rests on a noiseless simulation: the positive control at `v2_adversarial_reversal_recheck.py:146-159` builds paths that obey the law at the observed `R` and prints the gap from their slope to the observed one, which is a property of those constructed paths and not a measurement of the test's power on real data.
- Surrogate bands use a circular block bootstrap, fixed block length 20 (`opus_final_verify.py:190-195`).
- No saved Galperin forecast-scoring run in this evidence set uses the 90-day production horizon: the scoring scripts run 1, 5, 10 and 20 (`opus_forecast_gate.py:33`) and 7, 14 and 30 (`t4_results_prodhorizons.json` horizon keys, `v4_adversarial_recheck_galperin_timing.py:31`, `v4b_oracle_and_endpoint_bias.py:32`), so of the production pair 30 and 90 only 30 is scored here.
